A QUICK WORD ABOUT QUALITY

Smaller doesn't have
to mean a mystery.

There is no magic setting that makes every file dramatically smaller with zero quality loss. We show the trade-off before you start.

Lossless: keep the exact content

ZIP restores the exact original files. PNG lossless mode recompresses image data without changing pixels or metadata. Already compact files may stay the same size.

Visual quality: choose a smaller result

JPG, WebP and MP4 encoding trades some detail for fewer bytes. Start with High visual quality for important photos, or Balanced for everyday sharing. Always check the preview.

GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things to know.

Are my photos or videos uploaded?

No. This tool reads selected files in your browser and processes them on your device. The site downloads page assets and, only when needed, the video engine. It has no media upload endpoint.

Can I compress any file type?

Any file type can go into a ZIP within the displayed memory limit. Direct image conversion supports browser-decodable JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and BMP. Video compression attempts MP4, MOV, WebM and other FFmpeg-supported containers; unusual codecs or damaged files can fail. Use ZIP for GIF animation, SVG, unsupported images and exact originals.

Can I compress without losing quality?

Choose ZIP to preserve every byte, or lossless PNG to preserve the PNG's image data and metadata. Lossless image mode keeps other image formats unchanged. Video re-encoding and standard JPG or WebP conversion are lossy, even when the difference is hard to see.

Why isn't my file much smaller?

A file may already use efficient compression. Re-encoding can even make it bigger. For image and video compression, we keep your original when it is smaller. ZIP adds a little archive overhead and cannot guarantee a reduction.

How large a video can I process?

The tool shows a conservative file-size limit based on your device. It currently allows up to 100 MB on mobile or low-memory devices, and 200 MB on other devices. Even smaller files can exceed available memory. Keep the tab open; long or high-resolution videos can take several minutes. Use a desktop video app for very large projects.

Is it free? Why is there an advertisement?

The tools are free and do not need an account. Starting compression can show one clearly labelled sponsored-offer panel per browsing session. You can close it immediately. Opening the advertisement is optional and never required to finish or download.
