01 / THE USE CASE

Who is Anker 140W 4-Port USB-C Charger for?

03 / THE TRADEOFF

What could make it the wrong purchase?

A 140W total rating is not four separate 140W outputs. Sharing power can reduce laptop input when the other ports are occupied. Travellers should check the physical plug type and whether a local adapter is required. A compact 70W charger may be a better purchase if the laptop has modest charging needs.

Wired chargers: the compatibility check

Write down the charging input of each device and which ones must charge together. Read the maker’s power-sharing table for that exact combination. Include the right cable in the cost, and confirm plug style and mains compatibility for your destination. More advertised watts cannot make a device accept power beyond its supported input.
