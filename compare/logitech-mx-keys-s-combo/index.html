03 / THE TRADEOFF

What could make it the wrong purchase?

A bundle can look economical while duplicating a mouse or keyboard you already own. It also ties your choice of pointing device to the package. If a vertical mouse suits your hand better, buying individual items can be a better long-term decision despite the apparent bundle saving. Check layout language and the exact contents before ordering.

Keyboards and combos: the compatibility check

Verify the regional key layout, desk footprint and exact contents of a bundle. Compare connection support on every device you use, and check whether software features are permitted on a work computer. Typing feel and sound are personal. A compact, split, wave-shaped or mechanical board solves a different physical preference.
