03 / THE TRADEOFF

What could make it the wrong purchase?

The case adds cost and carrying weight to a tablet that may originally have been bought for simple reading or video. Consider whether a separate keyboard would be used just as often for less money. Check generation, connector arrangement and language layout in the seller description. A low price attached to an incompatible variation is not a meaningful saving.

iPad keyboards: the compatibility check

Find the tablet’s model number in Settings and match the seller’s exact compatibility list. Screen size alone is insufficient. Check keyboard language, connector arrangement and whether the package includes a trackpad. Compare the weight and support space needed for the whole tablet-and-keyboard setup, not just the accessory’s starting price.
