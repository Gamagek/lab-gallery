01 / THE USE CASE

Who is Logitech MX Anywhere 3S for?

The Anywhere 3S belongs on a shortlist for people who repeatedly work away from a permanent desk. A compact conventional shape can be easier to pack than a full-size productivity mouse and more familiar than a folding design. Think about how long you use the mouse at each stop. A portable item still needs to feel comfortable for the longest part of the working day.

03 / THE TRADEOFF

What could make it the wrong purchase?

Compact does not mean ideal for every hand. A larger grip may prefer the support of a desk-oriented model, while someone with a very thin travel sleeve may prefer a folding design. Check supported connections and whether any software customisation is available on your work computer. Do not pay for controls that policy prevents you from configuring.

Mice and trackballs: the compatibility check

Check handedness, grip, hand size and the supported connection method. Confirm that any customisation software can be installed on the intended computer. Physical comfort deserves a trial or a useful return window; it cannot be inferred from a premium price. Keep a familiar pointing device available while adapting to a new shape.
