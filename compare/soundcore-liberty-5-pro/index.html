03 / THE TRADEOFF

What could make it the wrong purchase?

In-ear comfort, background noise and software settings vary between users and situations. Check the exact features on your operating system, and read the return terms for an item worn in the ears. A premium case or extra controls should not outweigh comfort. Avoid confusing this model with Liberty 5 Pro Max or the standard Liberty 5 when comparing prices.

Audio: the compatibility check

Choose the physical listening format first: in-ear, open-ear, over-ear or a shared speaker. Check phone codec support, call controls and the listening mode used for any runtime claim. Fit and room noise change the result. Water-resistance ratings apply under specified conditions; read the maker’s care instructions before outdoor use.
