01 / THE USE CASE

Who is Logitech MX Brio 4K Webcam for?

A higher-resolution webcam can be a useful part of a desk where video is important, but it should be compared as a complete camera setup. Lighting, camera height and the meeting service affect what other people actually see. The MX Brio is worth considering when you have already addressed those basics and want more flexibility than an entry-level Full HD camera.

03 / THE TRADEOFF

What could make it the wrong purchase?

A 4K label does not mean every call will be transmitted in 4K. Application limits, bandwidth and settings can reduce the delivered resolution. Check your software and the exact camera mode before spending. For routine meetings, an inexpensive camera plus better lighting can be a more noticeable improvement than a premium camera in a dark room.

Webcams: the compatibility check

Start with lighting, camera height and the resolution supported by your meeting or recording application. Confirm the required USB connection and selected video mode. A higher camera resolution does not guarantee a higher-resolution call. Compare privacy controls and microphone arrangements as well as the camera headline.
